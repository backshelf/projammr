## Overall Impression

Bones are right: document does not scroll, one inner canvas with overscroll-y contain, dock owns the safe area, all dock targets ≥44px, long-press and haptics done with care, all inputs 16px. The mini-player → lava-field now-playing sheet is the emotional peak. Three behaviours give it away as a website: scroll position lost on back, soft keyboard pushing the comment box into the dock, and an installed PWA with a white splash, white status bar in dark mode and nothing offline.

## What's Working

- The dock is real phone chrome: one fixed container for mini-player and tab bar, safe-area inset on the container, `--dock` token every scroll container pads by.
- Touch craft: use-long-press.ts (drift slop, WebKit range drop, callout suppression, trailing-click swallow); delegated pointerdown haptics via the hidden-switch trick; global touch-action manipulation and transparent tap highlight.
- The now-playing sheet at 390px: full-width cover, lava field, version chip, LUFS scale, 64px play, 44px seek, swipe-to-dismiss with haptic. Identical on Pixel 7.

## Priority Issues

- [P0] Scroll position destroyed on every back navigation. Inner ScrollRoot/CANVAS scrolls, browser restoration never applies, nothing replaces it (verified 500 → 0). Fix: key offsets by history.state.key in packages/ui/src/components/layout/scroll-root.tsx and the canvas scroll hook; save on route change, restore before paint. $impeccable harden, $impeccable adapt
- [P0] No soft-keyboard handling; comment box under the dock. No visualViewport / interactive-widget / VirtualKeyboard anywhere. Focused textarea flush against the tab bar with clipped focus ring. Fix: interactiveWidget "resizes-content" in layout.tsx viewport, hide the dock while an input is focused, scroll compose into the visual viewport. $impeccable harden, $impeccable layout
- [P1] Installed, it is not an app. No service worker; manifest.ts background_color/theme_color light-only (white splash in dark mode); appleWebApp statusBarStyle "default" (white status bar over a dark app). Fix: black-translucent, dark manifest background, minimal offline shell. $impeccable harden, $impeccable polish
- [P1] Desktop editing surfaces never adapted. 530px dashed drag-and-drop dropzone on the project page; the track's own cover rendered inside the dashed box; settings help text in a four-character column and fifth tab clipped at "DANGE"; command palette ships "Toggle theme"/"Keyboard shortcuts ?" to touch; CommentCompose.tsx:102 prints "⌘+Enter to post". Fix: phone: variant collapsing the dropzone to "Add cover", single-column settings help, fine: gating on keyboard-only rows and hints. $impeccable adapt, $impeccable distill
- [P1] Native gestures invisible; destructive rows where the thumb lands. No sheet draws a grabber; "Delete" and "Sign out" are the bottom rows on the home-indicator strip. Fix: 36×4 grabber in bottom SIDE_CLASSES of sheet.tsx, destructive rows above a spacer or behind confirm, one-time hint on the mini-player. $impeccable clarify, $impeccable harden
- [P2] Track hero tap targets fail WCAG 2.2 AA 2.5.8: play 32×32, options 28×28, version switcher 185×16, LUFS 28×14, TP 14×14 (their FloatingNote is coarse:hidden so tapping does nothing). Fix: 44px coarse: hit areas; drop abbr handlers on touch or give a tap-to-open note. $impeccable audit
- [P2] Version picker is a desktop dropdown inside the now-playing sheet: anchored popover over the transport, one-line row with truncated filename, "make primary" star nested in a menuitem. Fix: bottom action sheet with two-line rows and a separate trailing primary toggle. $impeccable shape

## Persona Red Flags

Producer: 42vh coverless masthead + metadata grid before content; list resets to top after every track; "1 row" says nothing about what changed.
New collaborator: SEARCH tab opens a 13-item command palette ("New session", "Keyboard shortcuts"); never learns swipe-up / long-press; comment box half under the tab bar; /notes top strip says "Select a project" while the tab bar shows the project.
Screen-reader / motor-impaired: landmarks and labels broadly good; hero controls and LUFS/TP under 2.5.8; row action sheet's only non-gesture dismissal at the top while Delete is at the bottom; tab long-press has no AT equivalent; reduced motion honoured.

## Minor Observations

Arrival stagger inverts on first paint (body opaque, title still fading). Dock sets data-tabbar in an effect so --dock jumps once after hydration. html/body overscroll-behavior auto (iOS rubber-bands the empty document). Settings tab strip scrolls horizontally with no fade and conflicts with back-swipe. Stray > chevron on the Nightdrive row only. Row meta truncates mid-word. Account sheet has no close and no identity beyond a label. No perceptible route transition on a phone. Good: phone chrome is md:hidden CSS, no desktop→phone flip after hydration.
