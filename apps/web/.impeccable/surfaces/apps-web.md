Scope: the marketing pages of `apps/web` (landing, `/dit`, `/features/*`, `/pricing`, `/ai`). Visitor mode: Persuade. The docs under `/docs` are Read mode inside the same world and are not governed by this contract.

Audience and job: a producer with a small circle of collaborators, arriving from a link or a search, deciding whether Projammr is worth running. Primary actions: self-host (the docs quickstart), see pricing (hosted plans are "coming soon"), download dit. Proof is the product itself: the save diff, the waveform with timecoded pins, the loudness readout, real screenshots from the seeded demo catalogue. No invented testimonials, users, figures or benchmarks; prices are placeholders kept in one data file.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is a save-to-save diff of a producer's week. Every feature arrives as an addition to the record, set in dit's own diff grammar (gutter marks, hunk headers, additions in the art colour). It refuses the category default: a hero with a browser-framed screenshot, a three-card feature grid and a logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: The Listening Room from DESIGN.md, unchanged. True-neutral paper and ink with grain, glass and hairlines; the four covers are the only light, each blurred into its own masthead or thrown as a wash behind one hunk, never two hues in one band. Redaction 35 for the page's one h1, Departure Mono tracked caps for labels, hunk headers and the `+ ~ −` gutter, Spline Sans Mono for everything else. Additions in `--art`, removals in faint ink, no green. Readouts are the real `@workspace/ui` gear rendered statically.

STORY: The visitor sees that Projammr reads their Ableton saves and turns them into a history they can hear; then that review is listening-first, with timecoded feedback on every bounce; then that one place holds the song from a lyric idea to a finished collection, lit by its own artwork. Each claim is a hunk showing the real thing. They leave to self-host, to pricing, or to the dit download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The header in normal flow over a masthead lit by the blurred teal cover. Left half: the Redaction headline at display size, a one-line lede that opens on "open source and self-hosted", then the actions: "Self-host it" (ink, primary), "Pricing" (ghost), and a quiet "Download dit for Mac" link. Right half: the real save-diff component showing two saves of one of the demo catalogue's sets (tempo change, clips added, a plugin added, a track removed) with its gutter. On a phone the headline and actions come first and the diff sits under them.

FORM: The Session Diff, structure 7 of 7 on the ordered list, dealt as the lead by concept-seed key 5f17c077. Code-led (no image generation). Signature interaction: each hunk's lines tick in as the hunk enters the viewport, by CSS scroll-driven animation with no script; under reduced motion the lines are simply present.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
