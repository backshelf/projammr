Projammr gives you two ways to let someone else in, depending on how close they are to the work.

**Members** are the people you make music with: co-writers, band members, a mix engineer. You invite them into a
project, they get an account, and they see the whole project, with what they can change set by their role.

**A share link** is for everyone else: a label, a manager, a friend whose ear you trust. They get a link to one
track or one collection, open it on the share page, listen, and leave comments on the audio. They never need an
account and never see the rest of the project.

|                   | Members                       | Share-link visitors                 |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Needs an account  | Yes                           | No                                  |
| Sees              | The whole project             | One track or collection             |
| Can change things | Editors, admins and the owner | No                                  |
| Can comment       | Every member                  | Yes, on the audio, with just a name |
| How they arrive   | An invite                     | A link you send them                |

Everything a member does, and every comment, is recorded in the project's activity feed.
