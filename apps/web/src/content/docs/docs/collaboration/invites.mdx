An invite brings someone into a project with the role you choose. Admins and the owner can send one; the person
accepts it with an existing Projammr account or a new one.

## Invite someone

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** from the sidebar and choose **Project**, then **Members**.

2. Under **Invite by email**, enter their email address and choose their role. The form starts on **editor**;
   choose **viewer** for someone who should only listen and read, or **admin** for someone who should manage
   the members with you.

3. Choose **Invite**.

</Steps>

You can invite someone with a role up to your own, and not someone who is already a member.

The invite appears under **Pending invites** with its role, who sent it and when it expires. It lasts **seven
days**.

### Getting the invite to them

If your instance is set up to send email, Projammr emails the invite to the address you entered. If it is not,
or the email does not arrive, choose **Copy link** on the pending invite and send the link yourself.

<Aside type="caution">
  Anyone who opens an invite link and accepts it joins the project as whichever
  account they are signed in with, so send the link only to the person it is
  for.
</Aside>

### Cancel an invite

Choose the bin icon on a pending invite to revoke it. The link stops working at once, and anyone who opens it is
told it is not valid.

## Accept an invite

Opening an invite link shows a **Join** page for the project, naming who invited you and the role you are
invited as.

<Steps>

1. If you have no account on the instance, create one on that page; if you have one, sign in.

2. Choose **Accept invite**. You join the project and it opens.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  If the instance's registration is closed, you can still create an account from
  an invite, as long as you use the email address the invite was sent to.
</Aside>

## When an invite does not work

The invite page tells you what went wrong:

- **"This invite link isn't valid"**: it was revoked, or the link was cut short when it was copied.
- **"This invite has already been used"**: someone accepted it already.
- **"This invite has expired"**: it is more than seven days old.

In each case, ask whoever invited you to send a new one.
