Everyone in a project is a member with one role. The role decides what they can change; every member can open
and play everything in the project.

Membership is per project. There is no way to give someone one collection or one track and not the rest; for
that, send a [share link](/docs/collaboration/share-links/).

## The roles

Each role can do everything the one above it can, plus more.

| Role       | What it adds                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Viewer** | Open and play everything in the project; read its activity and member list; comment, and edit or delete their own comments.                                 |
| **Editor** | Create, change and delete collections, tracks and sessions; upload audio; create share links; push sessions and bounces into the project with [dit](/dit/). |
| **Admin**  | Invite people, change members' roles, and remove members.                                                                                                   |
| **Owner**  | Delete the project and hand ownership to someone else.                                                                                                      |

A project has one owner: whoever created it, until they transfer it.

Roles are checked by the server on every request, not just hidden in the app. Someone who is not a member of a
project cannot tell it exists: its pages answer as not found.

## See who is in a project

Open **Settings** from the sidebar and choose **Project**, then **Members**. It lists every member with their
role; admins and the owner also see the invite form and the pending invites there.

## Change a role

Admins and the owner can change a member's role with the menu on their row: **admin**, **editor** or
**viewer**. Two limits apply:

- You can give a role no higher than your own. An admin can make someone an admin; only the owner has the
  owner's actions.
- You cannot change the role of someone who outranks you.

## Remove a member

Admins and the owner can remove a member with the bin icon on their row. They lose access to the project
straight away. The same limits apply: you cannot remove someone who outranks you, and the owner cannot be
removed.

## Transfer ownership

The owner can hand the project to another member with the crown icon on that member's row. Projammr asks you to
confirm, because the change is not yours to undo: the other member becomes the owner, you become an admin, and
only the new owner can transfer it back.

<Aside type="caution">
  Transferring ownership also hands over deleting the project. Only transfer it
  to someone you trust with that.
</Aside>

Transferring is also how you make way to [delete your own account](/docs/guide/settings-and-profile/#deleting-your-account): Projammr will not delete an account that is
the only owner of a project other people are still in.

## Leave a project

Any member can leave a project, whatever their role. Press Leave on your own row in Settings → Project → Members, or choose
Leave project in the project menu at the top of the sidebar, and confirm. You lose access straight away and only
an invite gets you back.

The owner cannot leave while they are the only owner: [transfer ownership](#transfer-ownership) to another member
first, then leave.
