A share link lets anyone you send it to play one track or one collection on a public page, the share page, and
leave comments on the audio. They do not need an account, and they see nothing else in the project.

Editors, admins and the owner can create share links.

## Create a share link

<Steps>

1. Open the track or collection you want to share.

2. In its **Sharing** section, choose the **+** (**Share**) button.

3. Choose **what the link plays**:
   - **Current version** always plays whatever the current primary version is. When you add a new version and
     make it primary, the link plays the new one.
   - **Snapshot** freezes the versions you pick now, whatever changes later. You choose the version of each
     track, and can give the share its own title (and, for a collection, a title for each track) for the
     people who open it.

4. Set the options you want (below), then choose **Create link**.

5. Choose **Copy share link** on the new link and send it however you like.

</Steps>

### Options

- **Note for whoever opens it**: a message shown under the title, such as what you want them to listen for.
- **Stops working**: off (**Never**) to begin with. Turn it on and pick a date for the link to expire on.
- **Keep guest comments private**: each visitor sees only the comments they wrote and the replies to them.
  Project members still see every comment.
- **Allow downloads**: adds a download link beside each track on the share page. Off to begin with.

## Manage your links

Each link is listed in the **Sharing** section with its type, and for a snapshot how many tracks it holds, and
whether it expires: "Never expires", "Expires" with a date, or "Expired" with a date.

The menu on a link offers:

- **Edit**: change its options, and for a snapshot its versions and titles.
- **Make snapshot** or **Make connected**: switch between freezing the versions and following the current one.
- **Delete**: the link stops working immediately for everyone who has it. There is no undo; create a new link
  to share again.

## What someone sees on the share page

The share page is Projammr without the app around it. The artwork fills the page, and over it sit:

- whether it is a **Shared track** or a **Shared collection**, its title, and your note;
- a play button and the waveform, which they can click to jump through the audio, with the length beside it;
- for a collection, the list of its tracks, with controls to skip between them;
- a download link for each track, if you allowed downloads;
- the comments, with a note that anyone with the link and the project's members can see them.

## Commenting without an account

Anyone can read the comments on a share page. To write one, a visitor enters **Your name** and chooses **Continue
as guest**; that name is shown on everything they post. No email address or password is involved.

Comments on the share page belong to the audio, and one made at a moment in it shows as a mark on the waveform.
Guests can edit and delete their own comments.

The comments land on the version of the track they were made on, so project members see them in the app, in that
version's comments, alongside their own. A member who opens the share page while signed in comments as
themselves.

<Aside type="note">
  Guests are not accounts on your instance. They never appear in the instance's
  list of accounts, and they cannot see anything a share link does not show
  them.
</Aside>

## When a link stops working

Someone who opens a link that has expired is told "This share link has expired". A deleted link, or a link that
was cut short when it was copied, says "This share link isn't valid".
