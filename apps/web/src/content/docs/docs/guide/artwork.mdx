Projammr has no colour scheme of its own. Each project, collection and track is lit by its cover art: the
masthead, the glow behind the page and the player all take their accent from the record. Add a cover and the
page changes colour to match it.

## Adding a cover

Projects, collections and tracks each have an **Artwork** section on their page.

<Steps>

1. Open the page and find the **Artwork** section.

2. Drop an image onto it, or select **Add cover** and pick one. JPG, PNG, GIF and WEBP images work, up to the
   size limit your instance sets (128 MB unless whoever runs it has changed it). The uploader shows the limit.

3. Once the upload finishes, the page takes on the cover's colour.

</Steps>

To swap the cover, select **Replace** (or **Change**) and pick another image. To take it away, use the remove
button on the image; the page then falls back as described below.

You need to be an editor, admin or owner of the project to add, replace or remove artwork.

## Where a page gets its cover

Most tracks never get a cover of their own: they share their release's. So when a page has no artwork, Projammr
looks up the chain for the nearest one that does:

| Page       | Uses, in order                                                                                  |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Project    | Its own cover                                                                                   |
| Collection | Its own cover, then the project's                                                               |
| Track      | Its own cover, then its first collection's, then the project's                                  |
| Session    | Whatever its track would show. Sessions have no cover of their own                              |

A track's "first collection" is the oldest of the collections it is on, so a song on both an EP and an album shows
the same cover everywhere it appears. If you want it to show the album's cover instead, give the track that cover
itself.

If nothing in the chain has a cover, the page is shown in greys.

<Aside>
  The colour comes from the image, worked out once on the server when you upload it. Projammr keeps the colour
  readable in both themes, so a very pale or very dark cover still gives an accent you can see. A cover with
  hardly any colour in it, like a black-and-white photo, gives a grey page.
</Aside>

## Every image in the project

**Settings** → **Project** → **Images** shows every image uploaded to the project, with a preview of each. It is a place to
look, not to tidy up: to remove a cover, go to the page it belongs to.

## What else takes the cover

- **The player** and the now-playing sheet sit on a blurred copy of the cover of whatever is playing.
- **Share pages** put the cover of the shared track or collection behind the whole page.
- **The tracks table** shows each track's cover as a thumbnail, following the same chain.
