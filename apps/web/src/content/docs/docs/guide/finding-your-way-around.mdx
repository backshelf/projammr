On a computer, everything in Projammr hangs off the sidebar on the left: the project you are in, the latest of
its collections, tracks, notes and sessions, and your account. On a phone the sidebar makes way for a bar along
the bottom; [Projammr on your phone](/docs/guide/phones/) covers that layout.

## The project switcher

The top of the sidebar shows the project you have open. Select it to open the project switcher: "Your projects"
lists every project you belong to, and **Add New** starts a new one.

/>

Switching projects changes everything below it in the sidebar. Projammr remembers the project you last had open
and returns to it the next time you visit.

With a project open, two rows sit under the switcher:

- **Search**, which opens the [command palette](/docs/guide/search-and-shortcuts/), and beside it the jobs tray,
  where background work on your uploads shows its progress.
- **View project**, the project's own page.

Below them, on every page, **Settings** opens [Settings](/docs/guide/settings-and-profile/#settings): your own, the
current project's (its statuses, labels, images, assets, members and, for its owner, the danger zone) and, for
the instance's admins, the instance's.

## The sidebar groups

Below those rows the sidebar has four groups, each a shortcut to the latest few of one kind of thing:

| Group       | Shows                                                           | Its **+** makes | Its name opens                  |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Collections | Up to five of the project's collections                         | a collection    | every collection in the project |
| Tracks      | The five tracks edited most recently                            | a track         | every track in the project      |
| Notebook    | Your five most recently edited notes, even with no project open | a note          | the notebook                    |
| Sessions    | The five sessions edited most recently                          | a session       | every session in the project    |

Select a group's name to see everything of that kind, and the **+** beside it to make a new one. Each group folds
away with the arrow beside its name.

## Your account

The bottom of the sidebar shows your name. Select it for the account menu:

- **Profile** opens [your profile](/docs/guide/settings-and-profile/).
- **Settings** opens [Settings](/docs/guide/settings-and-profile/#settings), as the sidebar's own item does. The
  instance's admins find [the instance's settings](/docs/admin/) there too.
- **Sign out** signs you out of this browser.

The button beside your name hides the sidebar, giving the page the whole window. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd>
(<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> on a Mac) hides it and brings it back.

## Light and dark

Projammr follows your device's light or dark setting until you choose otherwise. Press <kbd>D</kbd> anywhere in
the app, outside a text field, to swap between light and dark. To go back to following your device, open the
[command palette](/docs/guide/search-and-shortcuts/) inside a project, type `theme` and a space, and pick
**System**.

<Aside>
  Artwork, not a theme colour, lights each page. In either theme a project, collection or track takes its accent
  from its own cover art; [Artwork and colour](/docs/guide/artwork/) explains how.
</Aside>
