To use Projammr you need an account on a Projammr instance: the copy of the app you, your band or your producer
runs. Depending on how the instance is set up, you create the account yourself or you come in through an invite.

## Signing in

Open the instance's address in your browser. If you are not signed in, the home page is the sign-in card: a
**Sign in** tab that takes your email and password and, when the instance lets you create an account, a
**Register** tab beside it.

<Screenshot
  id="sign-in"
  alt="The sign-in card, with email and password fields"
/>

An account is an email address and a password. There is no signing in with Google or any other service.

The heading on the card tells you how the instance takes new accounts:

| The card says                                                   | What it means                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Create the first account"                                      | Nobody has an account yet. The account you create here runs the instance: it becomes the instance's admin, and registration closes behind it, so nobody else can sign up uninvited. |
| "Sign in or create an account"                                  | Registration is open. Choose **Register** and give your name, your email and a password of at least 8 characters.                                                                   |
| "Sign in", with "Accounts on this instance are by invite only." | Registration is closed. You need an invite to a project, or an account the instance's admin makes for you.                                                                          |

Five wrong passwords within 15 minutes are allowed. The sixth locks the account for a minute, and each wrong
password after that doubles the wait, up to an hour. Signing in successfully clears the count.

### If you forget your password

What you do depends on whether the instance can send email.

- **It can:** the password field has a **Forgot password?** link. Enter your email on the "Reset your password"
  page, and Projammr emails you a link to choose a new password. The page says the same thing whether or not the
  address has an account, so nobody can use it to find out who is signed up.
- **It can't:** the sign-in card says "Ask your instance admin for a password reset link." [The admin can make a
  reset link](/docs/admin/accounts/#create-a-password-reset-link) for your account and pass it on to you.

Either link opens a page where you choose a new password of at least 8 characters, then sign in with it. A link
that has expired or been used says "This link is invalid or has expired."; ask for a new one.

## Joining a project from an invite

When someone adds you to their project, you get an invite link. Opening it shows the project's name, who invited
you, and [the role you will have](/docs/collaboration/members-and-roles/#the-roles) in the project.

<Steps>

1. Open the invite link. The page reads "Join", then the project's name.

2. If you are not signed in, sign in, or create an account on the **Register** tab. On an instance that is invite
   only, register with the email address the invite was sent to: an invite for that exact address is what lets a
   new account in.

3. Select **Accept invite**. Projammr says "You've joined the project" and opens it.

</Steps>

If the link does not work, the page says why:

- "This invite link isn't valid": it was revoked, or the link is incomplete.
- "This invite has already been used": somebody accepted it already.
- "This invite has expired": it is past its expiry date.

In each case, ask the person who invited you to send a new one.

## Your first project

Once you are signed in, the home page depends on how many projects you belong to:

- **None:** "No project open", with a **Create a project** button.
- **One:** Projammr opens it straight away.
- **Several:** a "Projects" list, one row per project with its cover, its number of tracks and when it last had
  any activity. Pick one to open it.

After that, Projammr remembers the project you have open and goes straight back to it the next time you visit.

### Creating a project

A project is an artist or a band: the home for its collections, tracks and sessions. If you release under more
than one name, give each name its own project.

<Steps>

1. Select **Create a project** on the home page, or **Add New** in the project switcher at the top of the sidebar.

2. Give it a **Name**. Everything else is optional: a **Description**, and **Links** to anywhere the project
   lives (a website, a streaming page, social media), each with a name and an address.

3. Select **Create project**. The new project opens, with you as its owner.

</Steps>

<Aside>
  **Projammr** is the self-hosted web hub — your catalogue, listening room,
  comments, notes and lyrics. A new project starts empty, and you can add
  collections, tracks and sessions by hand. To track Ableton Live sets,
  automatic saves, bounces and in-DAW actions from your Mac, pair it with
  [dit](/docs/dit/).
</Aside>
