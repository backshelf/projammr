Projammr is a home for unfinished music. It keeps a producer's catalogue in one place, with every bounce, version,
measurement, lyric and piece of feedback for each song beside it, and it reads the Ableton sets behind those songs
straight off your disk, so the history of the work is recorded without anyone typing it in.

It has three parts that share one look:

- **The web app**, where you and the people you work with keep track of songs, listen, comment and write. This
  guide is mostly about the web app.
- **The scanner**, a desktop app that watches your Ableton project folders and sends sessions and bounces to the
  web app once you have checked what it found.
- **Share pages**, public pages where someone outside a project can hear a song and comment on it through a share
  link, without an account.

## How your music is organised

Projammr organises music in four layers:

- **Projects** are artists or bands. Everything else lives inside one, and people join a project to work on it.
- **Collections** are what tracks ship as: albums, EPs, singles, or anything else you want to group.
- **Tracks** are songs. A track can be on several collections, or none. Each has its audio versions, lyrics,
  notes and feedback.
- **Sessions** are the DAW projects behind tracks: the Ableton sets, with their structure, plugins and every save.

[How the catalogue works](/docs/guide/catalogue/) explains each layer in full.
