Lyrics mode turns a note into a lyric sheet. Every line counts its syllables, lines that rhyme are marked and
coloured, and a couple of keys bring up rhymes and synonyms without leaving the page. The same tools work in the
notebook and in a track's **Lyrics** section.

## Turning lyrics mode on

New notes start in lyrics mode. For any other note, tick **Lyrics mode** in the note's **⋮** menu.

In lyrics mode every line is its own line, whether you press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd>
<kbd>Enter</kbd>, because the counts and rhymes work line by line. Switching a note into lyrics mode splits any
paragraphs into their lines for the same reason.

## Sections

Type a section name in square brackets on its own line, such as `[Verse 1]`, `[Chorus]` or `[Bridge]`, and it
becomes a section heading. You can also type <kbd>/</kbd> and choose **Section**.

Each section heading counts its lines and their average number of syllables, such as "8 lines · 7.5 avg", so you
can check that two verses scan the same way.

## Syllables

Each line shows its syllable count at the right. Projammr counts from a pronouncing dictionary. For a word the
dictionary does not know, such as a name or a made-up word, it guesses from the spelling and shows the count with a
`~` in front, such as "~7".

## Rhymes

Within each section, lines whose last words rhyme get the same letter beside their counts (A, B, C and on), so you
can read the rhyme scheme down the page. A capital letter is a perfect rhyme and a lowercase one a near rhyme. A
line that rhymes with nothing else in its section gets no letter. The letters start again at A in each section.

The rhyming word at the end of each line is underlined in a colour for its rhyme: a solid line for a perfect rhyme
and a dashed one for a near rhyme.

## Looking up rhymes and synonyms

While you write, type `\` and then a word to see what rhymes with it, or `~` and a word for other words that mean
the same.

- **Rhymes** list **Perfect** rhymes first, then **Near** ones, each with its number of syllables.
- **Synonyms** are grouped by part of speech, each with the meaning it comes from, so you can tell the senses of a
  word apart.

Pick one and it takes the place of what you typed. So typing `\night` and picking "light" leaves "light" in the
line.

<Aside>
  If you use `\` or `~` in your lyrics, change the keys that open these lists in
  [Settings](/docs/guide/settings-and-profile/#lyrics-suggestions), under **Editor**.
</Aside>

## Lyrics on a track

A track's page has a **Lyrics** section. With no lyrics yet, it offers:

- **+ Lyrics**, to start writing on the spot. The lyrics are a note in your notebook, linked to the track.
- **Link a note**, to make one of your existing notes the track's lyrics.

Once linked, the lyrics sit on the track's page with every tool above, and everyone in the project can read them.
The **⋮** menu there adds **Open in Notebook**. See
[Turning a note into lyrics](/docs/guide/notebook/#turning-a-note-into-lyrics) for who can change them.
