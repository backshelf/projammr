The notebook is yours alone: a place for lyric ideas, lines you overheard, plans for a record. Your notes do not
belong to a project, and nobody else can open them. When a note becomes a song's lyrics, you link it to the track,
and from then on the people in that track's project can read it too.

<Screenshot id="notebook" alt="The notebook: the list of notes on the left and an open note on the right" />

## Opening the notebook

Select **Notebook** in the sidebar, or press <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>N</kbd>. Your five most recently edited notes
are listed under it in the sidebar, even when you have no project open. On a phone, use the **Notes** tab at the
bottom.

## Writing notes

<Steps>

1. Select **New** above the list, the **+** beside Notebook in the sidebar, or press <kbd>N</kbd> then
   <kbd>N</kbd>. On a phone, press and hold the **Notes** tab.

2. Start typing. Nothing is saved until you do, so an empty note never clutters your list.

</Steps>

A note has no separate title field: **its first line is its title**, and its second line is the preview under the
title in the list. A note with nothing in it yet is called "New note".

The editor is a block editor, like many writing apps: type <kbd>/</kbd> at the start of a line for a menu of
blocks such as headings and lists.

Your writing saves itself a moment after you stop typing. The line at the top says where it is up to: "Saving…",
"Saved", "Unsaved" or "Couldn't save". If someone else changed the same note meanwhile, which can happen with
lyrics a project shares, it says "This note changed elsewhere" with a **Reload** button.

## Finding notes

The list puts pinned notes first, then the rest with the most recently edited at the top. "Filter notes…" narrows
it by title and preview; it does not search the rest of a note's text.

From the [command palette](/docs/guide/search-and-shortcuts/), type `note` and a space, then part of a note's
title, to jump to it from anywhere.

## The note menu

The **⋮** on an open note has:

- **Lyrics mode**, which turns on the [lyrics tools](/docs/guide/lyrics/). New notes start in lyrics mode.
- **Pin** or **Unpin**, to keep a note at the top of the list.
- **Copy as plain text**.
- **Link to track** or **Unlink from track**. See below.
- **Delete**, which asks first ("Delete" and the note's title). Only you can delete your notes.

## Turning a note into lyrics

Linking a note to a track makes it that track's lyrics. The note stays in your notebook, marked with the track's
name, and it shows on the track's page for everyone in the project.

<Steps>

1. Open the note and choose **Link to track** from its **⋮** menu.

2. Pick the project, then search for the track by title, artist, key or BPM, and pick it. As the window says,
   "The note becomes that track's lyrics, and everyone on the project can read it."

</Steps>

You can also start from the track: its **Lyrics** section offers **+ Lyrics**, to start writing new lyrics on the
spot, and **Link a note**, to pick one of your notes that is not linked yet.

A track has one lyrics note, and a note can be the lyrics of one track.

### Who can read and change lyrics

- Everyone in the project can read a track's lyrics.
- You, and the project's editors, admins and owner, can change them. The track's **Lyrics** section shows who
  wrote them when it was not you.
- Only you can link your notes to a track, and only you can delete them. You or an editor can unlink them.

A note you have not linked stays private to you.

## How your notes are stored

Projammr stores the text of your notes encrypted, and never sends the encrypted form out of the server. Someone
who gets hold of a copy of the database or its backups cannot read your notes from it. The server itself holds the
key, because it has to decrypt a note to show it to you, or to show a track's lyrics to the project. So this
protects your notes on disk; it is not end-to-end encryption.

<Aside>
  Whoever runs your instance runs the server that holds the key. On an instance you do not run yourself, treat
  your notes the way you would treat them in any other hosted app.
</Aside>
