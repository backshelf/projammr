Projammr works in your phone's browser, laid out for a small screen. It is at its best for listening, checking
where songs stand and leaving feedback on the move; the heavier editing, like working through a table cell by cell,
stays on a computer.

## Getting around

On a phone the sidebar is replaced by two bars.

- **Along the top**: the project you are in (tap it to switch projects), and on the right the jobs tray and your
  account. On your profile or a new project's page, it shows **Back** instead, since an installed app has no
  browser back button.
- **Along the bottom**: five tabs, **Collections**, **Tracks**, **Notes**, **Sessions** and **Search**. Search
  opens the [command palette](/docs/guide/search-and-shortcuts/). Press and hold **Notes** to start a new note
  straight away.

Your account menu has **Profile**, **Settings** (yours, the project's and, for the instance's admins, the
instance's), a theme switch (**light**, **dark** or **system**) and **Sign out**. The tabs along the bottom stay
in Settings too.

## Pages

Project, collection, track and session pages keep their shape: the cover-lit masthead, then the details, then the
page's sections. The details that sit in the rail on a computer come straight under the title, and the less-used
ones fold behind **More details**.

## Tables

Tables become lists of cards, each with a cover, the title, a couple of details, and its status and priority.

- **Tap the cover** to [peek](/docs/guide/editing-in-tables/#peek) at the row.
- **Tap ⋯, or press and hold a card**, for its actions, such as setting its status, adding it to a collection,
  moving it or deleting it.
- **Filter** opens "Filter & sort": the filters, the sort, grouping, your saved views, and **Select rows** to pick
  several cards and change them together with the bulk bar.

Cells cannot be edited on a phone, and there is no blank row for adding one: for those, open the row's page, or
use a computer.

## Listening

When something is playing, a small player sits above the bottom tabs, with a thin progress line. Tap it, or drag
it up, to open the now-playing sheet: the cover, the controls, the live loudness meter, the queue and
[the Lab](/docs/guide/the-lab/).

<Screenshot id="now-playing" viewport="phone" alt="The now-playing sheet on a phone, with the cover, the controls and the live loudness meter" />

- On a track, the version menu opens as a sheet from the bottom, titled "Versions".
- On iPhone and iPad the player has a mute button but no volume slider; use the phone's own volume buttons.
- Comparing two versions switches between them without matching their loudness. See
  [Comparing two versions](/docs/guide/waveform-and-comments/#comparing-two-versions).

<Aside type="caution">
  While the Lab is changing the sound, playback stops when the screen locks. The now-playing sheet warns you with
  "Lab on · playback stops when the screen locks". Turn the Lab off to keep listening with the screen locked.
</Aside>

On phones that support it, buttons give a short buzz when you press them.

## Installing Projammr on your home screen

Add Projammr to your home screen and it opens like an app: full screen, with its own icon, without the browser's
address bar.

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
<Steps>

1. Open your Projammr instance in Safari and sign in.

2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

3. Tap **Add**.

</Steps>
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android">
<Steps>

1. Open your Projammr instance in Chrome and sign in.

2. Open Chrome's **⋮** menu and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

3. Confirm.

</Steps>
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Projammr does not prompt you to install it; the browser's own menu is the way in.

## When you lose signal

Projammr needs a connection to load pages and to play audio. When the connection drops:

- a banner reads "Offline · changes wait for a connection", and what you were doing goes through once you are back
  online;
- opening a page you cannot reach shows "You're offline", with **Try again** for when the signal returns.

Covers you have already seen stay on your phone, so they still show while you are offline. Signing out clears them.
