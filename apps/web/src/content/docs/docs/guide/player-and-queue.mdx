The player keeps going while you move around the app, so you can listen to a mix while you read the comments on
it, change its status or look something up in another project.

## Playing something

Anything that can play has a play button: a track's masthead, a collection's masthead, each row of the tracks and
collections tables, and audio attachments. The **⋮** beside a play button chooses how it plays:

- **Play collection** plays a whole collection in its running order. On a track opened from a collection,
  **Play from here** plays the collection starting at this track. A track on its own just has **Play**.
- **Play next** puts it straight after what is playing now.
- **Add to queue** (or **Queue collection**) puts it at the end of the queue.
- **Download** saves a copy. See [Versions and uploads](/docs/guide/audio-versions/#downloading).

Playing a collection replaces the queue with its tracks, in order.

A track plays its primary version, unless you have picked another version on its page.

## The player

On a computer the player sits at the bottom of the sidebar, and floats in the corner when you hide the sidebar. On
a phone it becomes a strip above the bar along the bottom; see [Projammr on your phone](/docs/guide/phones/).

<Screenshot id="player-queue" alt="The player playing a track, with the queue open above it" />

Its controls, left to right: **Shuffle**, **Previous**, play and pause, **Next**, **Repeat** (select it to go
from off to repeating everything to repeating one), and **Mute** with the **Volume** slider. On iPhone and iPad
there is only **Mute**, because iOS sets the volume itself.

**More controls** holds **Speed**, and quick switches from [the Lab](/docs/guide/the-lab/): **Mono**, **Dim** and
**−14 LUFS**, with **Open Lab** for the rest.

## The queue

Select **Queue** on the player to see what is coming up, headed with how many tracks it holds.

- Drag a track by its handle to move it up or down the queue.
- Select a track to jump to it.
- The **×** on a track takes it out of the queue.
- **Clear** empties the queue.

## Now playing

The now-playing sheet gives the music the whole screen, with its cover behind everything. Open it with the
**Now playing** button on the player. It is headed "Now playing", or "Playing from" and the collection's name
when you are playing a collection.

<Screenshot id="now-playing" viewport="phone" alt="The now-playing sheet on a phone: the cover, the transport, and the live loudness meter" />

The sheet has three panes, switched by the buttons under the transport:

- **The player**: the cover, the title and artists, the live **Loudness** meter and, on a tall enough screen, the
  **Spectrum**. When the track has several versions, you can switch between them here, and the menu has **Go to**
  the track.
- **Queue**, as above.
- **Lab**, the [listening tools](/docs/guide/the-lab/).

## Keyboard shortcuts for playback

These work anywhere in the app while you are not typing:

| Key                                 | Does                     |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| <kbd>Space</kbd>                    | Play or pause            |
| <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd>         | Back or forward 5 seconds  |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Back or forward 30 seconds |
| <kbd>[</kbd> / <kbd>]</kbd>         | Previous or next in the queue |
| <kbd>M</kbd>                        | Mute                     |
| <kbd>L</kbd>                        | Open or close the Lab    |

The [command palette](/docs/guide/search-and-shortcuts/) plays things by name, too. Inside a project, type `play`,
`queue` or `playnext` and a space, then part of a track's title. While something is playing, `volume` takes a
percentage, `speed` a speed from 0.5 to 2, `pitch` a number of semitones from −12 to 12, and `seek` a time such as
`1:30`.

<Aside>
  Speed and pitch are for listening only. They change what you hear in this browser, never the file.
</Aside>
