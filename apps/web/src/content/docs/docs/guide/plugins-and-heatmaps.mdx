Because [dit](/dit/) reads your sets, Projammr knows two things you would otherwise have to keep track of by hand:
which plugins your music depends on, and when you actually worked on it.

## Plugins

A project page's **Plugins** section draws its sets on the left and their devices on the right, with a line for
every set that uses a device: up to eight sets and twelve devices. **All devices** opens the full inventory.

<Screenshot
  id="plugins"
  alt="The plugin inventory: a web of sets and the devices they use, above the list of devices"
/>

The inventory page, **Plugins**, opens with a count such as "64 devices across 23 sets · 41 third-party", then
a web of your sets and their devices, then the list. A set is a dot and a device a square, larger the more sets
use it. Sets settle beside the devices they share, and a line is brighter the fewer sets share its device, so
the gear that sets a set apart stands out from the gear every set has.

Point at a set to light the devices it uses, or at a device to light every set that uses it; a thicker line
means the set uses that device on more tracks. A note beside it names it and counts its sets or devices. Click
to keep it lit, and the note offers **Open set** or **Show row**; click it again, click an empty spot or press
Escape to let go. Drag to move around, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use the buttons at the web's top right to
zoom in, zoom out and fit it all back in. Once you have clicked into the web, the arrow keys move around, <kbd>+</kbd>
and <kbd>-</kbd> zoom, and <kbd>0</kbd> fits.

The devices in more than 40% of your sets (and in at least five of them), your stock chain, stay out of the web
so they do not bury the rest in lines. They are listed under it instead, most used first; **Show stock chain**
puts them in the web and **Hide stock chain** takes them out again.

Each device in the list shows:

- its name;
- who makes it and its format, such as "VST3" or "AU", or "Built-in" for a device that comes with the DAW;
- how many sets and how many tracks use it.

To narrow it, choose **All**, **Plugins** (third-party only) or **Built-in**, or type in "Search devices", which
also lights the matching devices in the web and brings them into view.

Select a device to see the sessions that use it, and on which of their tracks. That is the list to check before
you uninstall a plugin, move to a new computer, or send a session to someone who might not have it.

## Samples

**Samples** (from the command palette, "Go to samples") is the same page for the samples your sets use: a count
such as "142 samples across 12 sets · 18 shared by more than one · 3 missing", the web, and the list. The web
draws only the samples more than one set uses; a sample only one set uses is counted on that set's note as its
own. A sample dit could not find is an amber square. A sample shows its file name over its folder.
**Missing** narrows it to the files dit could not find on the computer that last pushed the set; samples from
Live's own library stay hidden until you turn **Library** on. Select a sample to see the sets that use it.

A session page's **Samples** section lists that set's samples with their state (`on the hub` with a one-file download,
`not pushed`, `outside the project folder`, or `missing`), and says how many other sets use each. When a set cannot
find its samples it marks them as missing, and when a set keeps samples outside its project folder it suggests Live's
**Collect All and Save**.

## Work heatmaps

A heatmap is a calendar with one square per day, shaded by how many saves dit recorded that day: one, two
or three, four to six, or seven and more. Hover a square for the date and the number of saves. Days follow your
browser's time zone.

There are three, from close up to the whole picture.

### Worked on, for a track

A track's rail has **Worked on**: the last twelve weeks of saves to its sessions, with a line such as "18 days in
the last 3 months · last save 2 days ago".

### Work, for a project

<Screenshot
  id="project-work-heatmap"
  alt="A project's work heatmap: a year of days shaded by how many saves each had, with the collection, label and DAW filters"
/>

The project page's **Work** section shows the last year, with a summary: how many saves on how many days, the
streak you are on, your longest streak, and when the last save was. It only appears once the project has saves.

Filter it by **Collection**, **Label** or **DAW** to see when a record, or one kind of song, got its work.

Select a day to list that day's saves underneath, headed "Saves on" and the date; **Show recent** goes back to the
latest saves. Each save shows what changed, the same way as a session's
[save history](/docs/guide/session-history/).

### Activity, across everything

Your [profile](/docs/guide/settings-and-profile/) has an **Activity** heatmap of the saves in every project you
belong to. Its summary counts the saves and the days, how many projects they were spread over, your longest
streak and when you last saved.
