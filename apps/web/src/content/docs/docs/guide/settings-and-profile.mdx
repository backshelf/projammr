Your profile holds everything about you: your name, avatar, email address and password, and where you are signed
in. Your preferences and a project's own settings are in **Settings**, covered at the end of this page.

Open your profile from the account menu: select your name at the foot of the sidebar and choose **Profile**. On a
phone, it is in the account menu at the top right.

## Name and photo

The name and photo in **Profile** are what everyone in your projects sees beside your comments and changes. Click
the photo, or drag an image onto it, to change it (**Change photo**), or **Remove** it. Change your **Name** and
select **Save**.

## Email

**Email** shows the address you sign in with, marked "(unverified)" if it has not been confirmed. Enter a **New
email** and save.

- **If your instance sends email**, Projammr sends a message to the new address, and the change happens once you
  confirm it there.
- **If it does not**, enter your **Current password** as well, and the change happens straight away.

## Password

Enter your **Current password** and a **New password** of at least 8 characters, and save. Projammr says "Password
changed".

## Signed-in devices

**Signed-in devices** lists everywhere your account is signed in, such as "Firefox on macOS", with "(this device)"
beside the one you are using and when each was last active. **Sign out** beside any other device signs it out. [dit
shows here](/dit/) by its own name, so you can sign it out too.

## Activity

Once you have saves in any of your projects, your profile shows an **Activity** heatmap of them across every
project. See [Plugins and work heatmaps](/docs/guide/plugins-and-heatmaps/#activity-across-everything).

## Deleting your account

**Delete account** tells you exactly what it will remove before you go ahead:

- projects only you are in, with everything in them;
- your comments, activity and notes.

Projects you share with other people stay, and your comments and activity in them are kept, credited to "Deleted
user".

If you are the only owner of a project that other people are still in, you cannot delete your account until you
make someone else its owner or remove the other members. The section lists those projects, each with a link to its
members.

<Steps>

1. Select **Delete account**. If your instance does not send email, enter your **Current password** first.

2. Type `DELETE` to confirm, and select **Delete account**.

3. If your instance sends email, Projammr emails you a link, and the account is deleted when you follow it.
   Otherwise it is deleted straight away.

</Steps>

<Aside type="danger">Deleting your account cannot be undone.</Aside>

## Light and dark

Press <kbd>D</kbd> to swap between light and dark, or use the command palette's `theme` command to follow your
device again. On a phone, the account menu has **light**, **dark** and **system** buttons. See
[Finding your way around](/docs/guide/finding-your-way-around/#light-and-dark).

## Settings

Select **Settings** in the sidebar or the account menu, or press <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>,</kbd>, from any page.
Settings opens on your own. The list beside them (above them on a phone) holds each section you can open:
**Personal**, which is yours wherever you sign in; **Project**, the current project's, while a project is open;
**Instance**, [the instance's](/docs/admin/), for its admins; and **Devices**, which holds browser-local settings and
your registered [dit](/dit/) machines (their open set, pushed repositories, "Never here" folders, "Not a bounce"
files, and Remove).

A setting saves as soon as you change it. The search box at the top finds a setting by name in every section, and
so does the [command palette](/docs/guide/search-and-shortcuts/): type a setting's name and choose it to open
Settings at that setting.

### Lyrics suggestions

While you write lyrics, `\` opens a list of rhymes and `~` a list of synonyms. If you need either character in
your lyrics, choose different ones under **Editor** in your personal settings: **Rhyme prefix** and **Synonym
prefix**. See [Writing lyrics](/docs/guide/lyrics/#looking-up-rhymes-and-synonyms).

### A project's settings

Each project has its own settings: open Settings with the project open and choose **Project**. Its pages are:

| Page           | Holds                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Statuses       | The project's statuses. See [Statuses, labels and priority](/docs/guide/statuses-labels-priority/#statuses).                                                  |
| Labels         | The project's labels. See [Statuses, labels and priority](/docs/guide/statuses-labels-priority/#labels).                                                      |
| Priorities     | The project's priority scale. See [Statuses, labels and priority](/docs/guide/statuses-labels-priority/#priority).                                            |
| Relation types | The project's track relation types. See [Relations, attachments and links](/docs/guide/relations-attachments-links/#relations-between-tracks).                |
| Fields         | Custom fields defined for the project's entities.                                                                                                             |
| Images         | Every image uploaded to the project. See [Artwork and colour](/docs/guide/artwork/#every-image-in-the-project).                                               |
| Assets         | Storage, dit repositories and every file in the project. See [Every file in the project](/docs/guide/relations-attachments-links/#every-file-in-the-project). |
| Members        | [Who is in the project, and their roles](/docs/collaboration/members-and-roles/).                                                                             |
| Danger zone    | Deleting the project, for its owner only. See [Deleting a project](/docs/guide/creating-and-editing/#deleting-a-project).                                     |
