A track's page opens on the waveform of the version you are looking at. It is where you listen, where feedback
gets pinned to the exact moment it is about, and where you set two versions side by side.

## The waveform

Click anywhere on the waveform to play from there, or drag along it to scrub. If nothing is playing yet, clicking
it starts the track.

With the waveform selected, the keyboard moves the playhead:

| Key                             | Moves                  |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd>     | 5 seconds back or on   |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> with an arrow  | 30 seconds back or on  |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> | the start or the end   |

Under the waveform, strips can show the song's loudness, its spectrogram and where it changed since the last
version. See [Loudness, spectrum and measurements](/docs/guide/loudness-and-spectrum/#the-strips-under-the-waveform).

## Timecoded comments

Comments on a version belong to that version, and each can be pinned to a moment in it. They show as small marks
above the waveform, so you can see at a glance where the feedback is.

<Screenshot
  id="track-comment-pin"
  alt="A comment mark above the waveform, hovered to show who left it, when in the song, and the start of what they said"
/>

- **Hover a mark** to see who left it, the time it is pinned to, and how it starts.
- **Select a mark** to jump the playhead there and open the comment.

### Leaving a comment on a version

<Steps>

1. Play the version, and pause or keep playing at the moment you want to talk about.

2. Open the version's comments from the button at the right of the line under the waveform, such as "3 comments
   on v2". A version with none says "Comments on v2".

3. Start writing. The stamp beside the box reads "At 1:04", the time the playhead was at when you started, and
   pins the comment there. Select the stamp to make it a general comment about the whole version instead.

4. Select **Comment**.

</Steps>

Every comment with a time shows it as a stamp; select the stamp ("Jump to this point") to play from there. A reply
belongs to the same moment as the comment it answers.

Switching to another version switches to its comments. For comments about the song as a whole, use the track's own
**Comments** section further down the page; see [Comments and activity](/docs/guide/comments-and-activity/).

<Aside>
  Anyone in the project can read and post comments, viewers included. You can edit or delete your own.
</Aside>

## Comparing two versions

Compare lets you flip between two versions at the same point in the song, so you hear exactly what a new mix
changed.

<Screenshot
  id="track-compare"
  alt="Comparing two versions of a track: both waveforms, the A/B switch, Match loudness, and where the two differ"
/>

<Steps>

1. On the track, select **Compare** at the right of the line under the waveform, and pick the version to compare
   with.

2. Projammr gets both ready, showing "Preparing comparison" with its progress.

3. Switch between them with the **A/B** switch, or press <kbd>V</kbd>. The playhead stays where it is.

4. Select the **×** to finish. If you finish on B, B keeps playing and becomes the version the page shows.

</Steps>

While comparing:

- **Match loudness** plays the two at the same loudness, so the louder one does not just sound better. Both
  versions need a loudness measurement first.
- **The change strip** under the waveforms shows where the two differ, second by second.

<Aside>
  On a phone, Compare switches between the versions without matching their loudness, and says "Levels not matched"
  (with how much louder B is, when it knows). The change strip and the Lab are not available while comparing
  there.
</Aside>
